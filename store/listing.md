```

FIND WHAT YOU NEED
• Browse Vehicles, Property, Electronics, Home & Furniture, Fashion, Jobs, Services, Pets, Boats & Marine and Lost & Found

SAFE AND LOCAL

Pacific Listing is made for the Pacific, by people who live here.
```

## Content rating questionnaires

Answer honestly. For a marketplace like this the answers are usually:
- **User-generated content / users can interact:** Yes. People post ads and contact each other.
- **Users can share their location:** No. People type a town; the app doesn't use GPS.
- **Digital purchases:** No in-app purchases. Promotions and plans are paid outside the app by M-PAiSA, MyCash or bank transfer.
- **Violence, gambling, adult content:** None. Prohibited by the Terms.
- **Contains ads:** **Yes.** The site shows banner ads from advertisers.

## Privacy / data safety answers

**Data collected** (all for "App functionality" and "Account management"; none sold; none used for tracking; all sent over an encrypted connection; people can delete their account in the app):

| Data | Collected | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | Shown on their ads |
| Email address | Yes | For login; never shown publicly |
| Phone number | Optional | Shown on ads only if the seller adds it |
| Photos | Yes | Photos the user adds to their ads or profile |
| Other user content | Yes | Ad text, chat messages between members, ratings and comments, saved searches, reports, contact messages |
| Other personal info | Optional | ID document photo, only if someone asks to be verified; deleted after checking |
| Purchase history | Yes | Promotions/plans they buy (no card details are stored) |
| Approximate location | Yes | Country only, from the internet connection (no GPS): sets local prices and counts visitors per country. Not shared. Purposes: App functionality, Analytics |
| Device or other IDs | No | |

**Data shared with third parties:** No. Supabase (database) and Vercel (hosting) are service providers processing data on our behalf.
